WHO STANDS WITH WENDY — AND WHY IT MATTERS TO EVERY ALAMEDA COUNTY VOTER

Trusted by organizations that fight for you

These endorsements come from organizations that answer to their members — not to party bosses or donor PACs. Each one represents a community of Alameda County voters who are demanding real accountability. They endorsed Wendy because her record and her platform earned it.

HOWARD JARVIS TAXPAYERS ASSOCIATION (HJTA PAC)
Official PAC Endorsement
California’s most powerful nonpartisan taxpayer watchdog — protecting Proposition 13 and fighting tax increases since 1978

The Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association does not endorse candidates as a favor. It endorses candidates who have demonstrated — in writing, on the record, and through a rigorous questionnaire process — that they will protect Proposition 13, oppose new taxes, and put the interests of California taxpayers before the interests of government.

Wendy Huang received the HJTA PAC’s official endorsement for California’s 14th Congressional District. That is not a small thing.

The HJTA is nonpartisan. It endorses Democrats when they earn it and rejects Republicans when they don’t. Its endorsement is a declaration that Wendy has signed the HJTA Pledge to Stand Up for Taxpayers, committing to support Proposition 13 and the Right to Vote on Taxes Act — and that the HJTA PAC board believes she will keep that commitment in Washington.

Proposition 13 is the foundational protection that has kept millions of California homeowners — including seniors living on fixed incomes — from being taxed out of the homes they spent their lives building. Sacramento politicians have been quietly chipping away at it for decades. Wendy’s HJTA endorsement is a guarantee that she will go to Washington as a defender of that protection, not another politician who looks for ways around it.

Why this matters to every Alameda County homeowner and renter:

Alameda County has some of the highest property taxes in California. Assessments have risen steadily, utility fees have multiplied, and the hidden taxes buried in every government program keep coming. The HJTA PAC endorsement signals that Wendy will oppose every attempt to raise your tax burden at the federal level and will fight to ensure you have the right to vote before any new tax is imposed. That protection belongs to every Alameda County family — whether you own or rent, whether you are a lifelong Republican or have never voted Republican in your life.

MOMS FOR LIBERTY
Endorsement
A national parental rights movement with chapters across California

Moms for Liberty was founded by parents who decided they were done being told to be quiet. Their endorsement of Wendy Huang reflects a shared conviction: that the people closest to a child — not the government, not a bureaucracy, not a school board — should make the decisions that shape that child’s future.

As a mother and an immigrant who built her American life through education and hard work, Wendy does not treat parental rights as a culture war skirmish. She treats it as a core economic issue: when families cannot trust their schools, when curriculum replaces rigor with ideology, and when parents are shut out of decisions, the result is a generation of children less prepared to compete, less equipped to succeed, and less free to think for themselves.

California spends more per pupil than nearly any other state and produces academic outcomes that should embarrass every incumbent who has held office in the past decade. Wendy Huang came to this country at age 13, speaking no English, and built a career as a high-tech executive through the discipline and opportunity that a real education provides. She is running for Congress so that every child in CA-14 has access to that same foundation — regardless of their zip code.

Why this matters to every Alameda County parent:

Moms for Liberty’s endorsement signals that Wendy will fight to give parents — not politicians — meaningful control over their children’s education. School choice, curriculum transparency, and academic accountability are not radical ideas. They are the minimum that every family deserves. Wendy will take that fight to Washington.

REFORM CALIFORNIA
Personal Endorsement
California’s leading government accountability and taxpayer advocacy organization

Carl DeMaio has spent more than two decades holding California politicians accountable — Democrats and Republicans — for wasteful spending, broken promises, and a political culture that protects insiders at the expense of taxpayers. His personal endorsement of Wendy Huang carries weight precisely because he does not give it freely.

Reform California’s mission is exactly Wendy’s mission: government that works for the people who pay for it, not the politicians who run it. DeMaio has taken on the most powerful political machines in Sacramento and won — not by playing the inside game, but by telling the truth loudly and refusing to back down. He recognized in Wendy a candidate who operates the same way.

Wendy’s platform — lower costs, real public safety, and economic opportunity — is inseparable from the accountability agenda that DeMaio and Reform California have championed across party lines for years. Both refuse to accept the argument that California’s dysfunction is inevitable. Both believe the problem is not the people of this state — it is the politicians who have run it into the ground while collecting their pensions and protecting their donors.

Why this matters to every Alameda County taxpayer:

Reform California holds politicians of both parties accountable for how they spend your money. Carl DeMaio’s personal endorsement signals that Wendy is not another career politician who will say one thing to get elected and vote differently in Washington. It is a seal of approval from California’s most credible government watchdog — and it was given because Wendy earned it.

REPUBLICAN NATIONAL HISPANIC ASSEMBLY OF SAN DIEGO (RNHASD)
Formal Endorsement
The San Diego County chapter of America’s oldest and only Hispanic Republican organization officially affiliated with the Republican National Committee — founded in 1967

The Republican National Hispanic Assembly of San Diego is not a rubber-stamp organization. Founded in 1967, it is the oldest Hispanic Republican organization in America with an official affiliation to the Republican National Committee. When it formally endorses a candidate, it is making a statement that carries more than 50 years of credibility behind it.

On May 5, 2026, the RNHASD formally endorsed Wendy Huang as the Republican candidate for California’s 14th Congressional District — calling on Republican, Democrat, and independent voters alike to support her. Their endorsement statement was unambiguous: “This is not a partisan appeal. It is an appeal to every voter who believes that the people they elect should be the people who represent them — not the insiders who disapprove of their choices.”

The RNHASD endorsed Wendy because they recognize her story. As an immigrant who came to this country and built a life through hard work, education, and perseverance, Wendy embodies the values that Hispanic families have brought to America for generations: faith, family, enterprise, and a refusal to accept that the system cannot change.

Their endorsement described Wendy as exactly the kind of leader CA-14 needs: a retired tech executive who understands how to build and innovate, a community advocate who prioritizes public safety and lower costs, and a candidate backed by reform-minded organizations because she is dedicated to restoring accountability to how taxpayer dollars are spent.

The RNHASD also addressed the broader moment directly: “The Hispanic community of San Diego and the Bay Area knows this story. Political machines that believe they are too powerful to be held accountable. Leaders who use institutional authority to silence voices they find inconvenient. Citizens who are told their votes do not count. The RNHASD was founded to fight exactly this kind of institutional exclusion — and we recognize it when we see it. Wendy Huang fought that fight on behalf of the voters of Alameda County. The RNHASD is proud to stand with her as she takes it to Washington.”

Why this matters to every Alameda County voter:

Alameda County’s Hispanic community is one of the largest and fastest-growing voter blocs in CA-14. The RNHASD’s endorsement — from an organization whose mission explicitly crosses partisan lines — signals that Wendy’s platform of accountability, affordability, and public safety resonates far beyond Republican party registration. When the oldest Hispanic Republican organization in America calls Wendy’s campaign “not a partisan appeal” but a civic one, every voter in this district should take note.

CALIFORNIA RIFLE AND PISTOL ASSOCIATION (CRPA)
Grade “A” Rating
California’s oldest and largest gun rights organization

The CRPA awards its Grade “A” rating only to candidates who demonstrate an unwavering commitment to the constitutional rights of law-abiding citizens. It is not handed out for showing up — it is earned by standing firm when it is politically inconvenient to do so.

Wendy earned this rating because she understands what every Alameda County resident living with rising crime rates already knows: when seconds count, the police are minutes away. The Second Amendment is not a partisan talking point — it is a safety net for families who have been left behind by failed public safety policies.

Sacramento has spent years passing laws that restrict the rights of law-abiding gun owners while career criminals face reduced sentences, early releases, and prosecutors who decline to charge. The result is a community where the people who follow the rules are penalized and the people who break them face minimal consequences. Wendy will reverse that equation.

Why this matters to you — regardless of whether you own a firearm:

A Grade “A” from the CRPA signals that Wendy will oppose legislation that criminalizes law-abiding behavior while doing nothing to stop actual criminals. In a county where crime has surged and prosecution has softened, that distinction is everything. Public safety is not a gun issue. It is a families issue. And Wendy’s record on this question is unambiguous.

A NOTE ON ENDORSEMENTS

Wendy has said publicly that the most important endorsement comes from the people themselves. “I’d rather get endorsements from the people,” she has said. “Their vote is their endorsement.”

The organizations above share Wendy’s values not because they were courted, but because her record and her platform earned their respect. The Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association is nonpartisan — it endorses any candidate, of any party, who will protect taxpayers. Moms for Liberty fights for children, not for parties. Reform California holds everyone accountable. The Republican National Hispanic Assembly of San Diego made clear that their endorsement is a civic appeal, not a partisan one. The CRPA defends law-abiding citizens regardless of their voter registration.

What unites all five endorsements is a single standard: results for the people, not protection for the powerful.

Wendy is not collecting endorsements to build a resume. She is building a coalition of Alameda County voters — parents, homeowners, taxpayers, gun owners, immigrants, small business owners, and anyone who is tired of being failed by politicians on both sides — who are ready to demand something better.

Remember to vote for Wendy Huang on June 2nd, 2026, at your polling place or now via your mail-in ballot. Let’s build a safer, more affordable, and prosperous tomorrow for our families and community.
