FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Date: June 3, 2026

From: Wendy Huang, CA14 Candidate & Duly Elected Alameda County GOP Central Committee Member

RE: Campaign Update, Transparency, and Media Availability

To all media outlets and CA-14 Republican, Democratic, Libertarian, and Independent voters,

I am fully committed to our June 2nd primary and June 16th special primary to fight for our voters here in Alameda County.

As I await the final vote count tonight, I am incredibly proud and grateful to every Democratic, Republican, Libertarian, and independent voter who cast their ballot for our campaign. Your support means everything to me, and I promise that I will not let you down.

Media Availability Update

I am deeply humbled by the attention our campaign is receiving. Due to the overwhelmingly high volume of interview requests from media outlets, I am respectfully requesting that all questions be submitted via email at this time. I am eager to speak with the press and share my vision, and I will be scheduling full in-person and on-camera interviews at a later date once the immediate rush has subsided.

A Call for Transparency in CA-14

I also want to raise an urgent issue that I believe voters deserve to know about. Many voters have shared their concerns with me that Dena Maldonado, is acting as a “ghost candidate”—placed on both ballots not to win, but to draw votes and manipulate the outcome of this election.

Voters deserve the full picture regarding the following facts before June 16th:

  • Maldonado’s FEC record shows zero fundraising after 90 days.
  • Her campaign mailing address is located in Dublin—which is completely outside of our district.
  • She has intentionally hidden her Alameda County GOP and State Republican Party endorsements from her own campaign website.

Unfortunately, these tactics directly mirror the ghost candidate scandal involving former Miami Republican state senator Frank Artiles in 2020.

My Commitment to You

I am fighting hard through June 16th and beyond. When we sit down for our in-person interviews later this week, I would love to share more on camera about the core issues that brought me into this race:

  • Establishing a Department of Technology featuring elected technology heads at the municipal, county, and state levels, as well as a Senate-confirmed federal Secretary of Technology.
  • Implementing special area codes for minors as a genuine, actionable child safety measure.
  • Demanding full public disclosure of the Epstein files, accompanied by a coordinated reinvestigation led by all state Attorneys General.
  • Advocating for the full reopening of US and Iranian Embassies to facilitate honest, transparent, accountable, and legally binding peace talks for peace and prosperity.

Thank you for your time, your questions, and your patience as we manage these interview requests. I am happy to say more soon.

Warmly,

Wendy Huang CA14 Candidate & Duly Elected Alameda County GOP Central Committee Member

I am eager to address your inquiries and scheduling full in-person and on-camera interviews at a later date once the immediate rush has subsided. Submit all questions here.
